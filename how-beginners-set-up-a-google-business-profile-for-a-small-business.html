Create Your Account and Add Your Business Name

You start with a Google Business Profile business name and category by opening the Google Business Profile site and signing in with your Google account. You learn how to set up Google Business Profile for beginners by typing your real business name exactly as customers know it. You pick the option that matches a shop, a service, or an online business.

A real name works because customers search for the name they heard from a friend or a signboard. You avoid extra words that promise services you never offer. You check the spelling twice and you keep the name the same on your shop sign and your profile.

Ayesha from Lahore shows this idea in action with a profile for her henna studio. She types her studio name exactly as it shows on her shop board. She picks the service option because she visits clients at home and she adds her service area next.

You pick the Google Business Profile business name and category with care by choosing the one category that names your main service. You add extra categories only when they name a real service you offer each week. You list your services in plain words so customers see what you do at a glance.

A right category works because Google shows your profile when nearby people search for that service. You earn more calls when the category matches the search words of real customers. You check the categories of similar shops in your city and you pick the one that fits your work best.

Verify Your Business With Google

You follow Google Business Profile verification for beginners by choosing the method Google offers for your business. You learn how to set up Google Business Profile for beginners by reading each instruction on the screen with care. You enter the code that Google sends and you wait for the profile to turn live.

Verification works because it proves to Google that the business belongs to you. You gain the power to edit the profile and answer reviews once Google confirms you. You never share your code with anyone and you keep your business documents ready in case Google asks for proof.

Sana from Islamabad shows this idea in action with a video call that verifies her tailoring shop. She shows her shop sign and her work tools on the call and Google confirms her business the same day. She checks her profile the next morning and she sees it live on Maps.

Add Photos Hours and a Short Description

You use Google Business Profile hours photos and reviews by adding clear photos of your shop, your work, and your team. You set your opening hours for each day and you mark the days when you stay closed. You write a short description that names your service, your city, and what makes your work worth a visit.

Fresh photos work because customers trust a shop they can see before they visit. You add new photos each month and you keep the old ones that still show your real work. You check the hours after a holiday and you fix them at once so no customer arrives at a closed door.

Usman from Faisalabad shows this idea in action with photos of his cloth shop and his best fabrics. He adds his opening hours for each day and he writes a short description in plain words. His customers call before they visit and they ask for the fabrics they saw in the photos.

Ask for Reviews and Reply to Every Customer

You grow Google Business Profile for local customers by asking happy customers for a short review after each visit. You send the review link on WhatsApp and you thank each person who leaves a review. You reply to every review with a warm line that names the service they used.

Reviews work because new customers trust the words of past customers more than any ad. You earn more calls when your profile shows fresh reviews with real names. You answer a bad review with calm words and you offer to fix the problem so other readers see that you care.
