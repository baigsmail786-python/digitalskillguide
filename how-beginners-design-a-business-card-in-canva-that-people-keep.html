How Beginners Design a Business Card in Canva That People Keep

Short answer: You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners by picking the size, adding your name and contact details, and exporting a PDF for print. You keep the design clean, and the reader finds your number in seconds.

Most beginners fill the card with every service and every social link, and the card turns into a crowded flyer. The reader then keeps nothing, and the card lands in the bin.

You will build a card people keep, and you will add a QR code that opens your shop WhatsApp or website. You will test one card, fix what reads badly, and print the batch.

Set the Size and the Layout Before You Add a Word

You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners fastest when you set the size first and leave the words for later. You open Canva, and you search for Business Cards to see the standard size. A tailor in Rawalpindi set the size first, and his card printed exactly as it looked on screen. You keep the name big, the number clear, and the edges free of clutter.

Pick the Standard Card Size in Canva

You pick the right business card size in Canva for beginners by typing Business Cards into the search box. Canva opens a blank card at the size printers expect, and you never guess the numbers yourself. You check the size in the top bar before you add text, and you fix it at once when it looks wrong. You use the same size for every card you make later.

Leave Space Around the Edges

You leave space around the edges, because printers trim a thin strip from each side. You keep your name, your number, and your logo well inside the border. You use the margin guides in Canva to see the safe zone, and you move any text that touches the guide. You print one test card first, and you check that nothing sits too close to the edge.

Choose Fonts and Colours That Match Your Business

You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners well when you match the fonts and colours to your business. A bakery in Lahore uses one warm colour and one round font on every card. You pick one font for your name and one for the details, and you never mix three fonts on the card. You pick colours that stay easy to read, and you test them on a phone screen before you print.

Pick Two Fonts and Use Them the Same Way

You pick two fonts for the card, and you use the larger one for your name and the smaller one for your details. You choose a font that reads well at small size, and you avoid thin fonts that blur in print. You set the name large enough to read from an arm length away, and you use the same two fonts on every card.

Choose Colours That Stay Easy to Read

You choose colours that stay easy to read, because a card that looks pretty but reads badly fails its only job. You use dark text on a light card, and you save bright colours for a small accent such as a line or a logo. You keep the colours the same across your card and your shop sign, and you read How Beginners Design a Logo in Canva That Looks Professional to match the card with a logo that suits it.

Add Your Name Logo and Contact Details

You add your name, your logo, and your contact details next, because the reader must know who you are and how to reach you. A plumber in Faisalabad puts his phone number in large text on the front, and customers call him straight from the card. You put your name at the top in the largest text, and you add only the details people use.

Put the Name and the Job First

You put your name and your job first, and you keep both in the largest text on the front. You place your phone number next, because most people call before they visit, and you place your town after it. You skip a fax number or a long address, and the card stays clean enough to read at a glance.

Add a QR Code That Opens Your WhatsApp or Website

You add a QR code to the card, because a code turns a paper card into a digital card that works after the meeting ends. A soap seller in Multan added a code that opens her WhatsApp chat, and customers now message her straight from the card. You open the Apps section in Canva, and you paste the link to your WhatsApp chat or your website to make the code.

Export the Card as a PDF and Print It Right

You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners best when you export the card the right way and check it before you print. A designer in Karachi exported a small image first, and the print came out blurry, so she switched to PDF Print and the next batch came out clean. You click Share and then Download, and you pick PDF Print so the printer gets a sharp file.

Export a Print Ready PDF From Canva

You export a print ready PDF from Canva by clicking Share and then Download and choosing PDF Print from the list. You tick crop marks and bleed when your printer asks for it, and you leave it off when the design stays inside the safe zone. You name the file with your business name and the date, and you open the PDF once to check every word.

Check One Printed Card Before You Print the Full Batch

You check one printed card before you print the full batch, because a test card costs little and a batch with a mistake costs far more. You read the name and the number on the test card, and you scan the code with your own phone to confirm it opens the right page. You keep the same colours and fonts on the card and the flyer, and you read How Beginners Design a Flyer in Canva for a Local Business to reuse the layout on a larger page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a business card be in Canva?

A business card in Canva uses the standard size of three and a half by two inches, and Canva sets that size when you search for Business Cards. You check the size in the top bar before you add any text, and you fix it at once when it looks wrong. You keep the same size for every card you print, and the cards stack neatly in a wallet.

Do I need Canva Pro to design a business card?

You do not need Canva Pro to design a good business card, because the free plan carries templates, fonts, and photos that suit most small businesses. You start with the free plan, and you learn its tools first. You upgrade only when you need a brand kit or a background remover every week.

What should I put on a business card?

You put your name, your job or shop name, your phone number, and one way to reach you online on the card. You add a QR code that opens your WhatsApp chat or your website, and you leave the rest off. You keep the card clean, and the reader finds your number in seconds.

How many fonts should a business card use?

A business card should use two fonts at most, and you use the larger one for your name and the smaller one for the details. You choose fonts that read well at small size, and you avoid thin or curly fonts that blur in print. You use the same two fonts on every card, and the cards start to look like one business.

How do I add a QR code to a business card in Canva?

You add a QR code in Canva by opening the Apps section and choosing a QR code tool. You paste the link to your WhatsApp chat or your website, and Canva places the code on your card. You test the code with your own phone before you print, and you make it large enough to scan from a short distance.

Should I put my photo on a business card?

You put your photo on a business card when people need to remember your face, such as a tutor, a coach, or a home service worker. You use a clear photo with good light, and you place it on the back of the card so the front stays clean. You skip the photo when the shop name matters more than the face, and the logo takes the front instead.

How do I export a business card from Canva for print?

You export a business card from Canva by clicking Share and then Download and choosing PDF Print from the list. You tick the crop marks and bleed option when your printer asks for it, and you check the PDF once before it reaches the printer. You print one test card first, and you order the full batch only when the test reads well.

How do I learn to design a business card in Canva as a beginner?

You learn by finishing one real card for your own business, and you start with the standard size and two fonts. You add your name, your number, and a QR code, and you export a PDF for print. You then test one printed card, fix what reads badly, and reuse the layout for the next card.
