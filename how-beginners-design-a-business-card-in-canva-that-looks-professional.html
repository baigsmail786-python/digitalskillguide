How Beginners Design a Business Card in Canva That Looks Professional

Graphic Design · 13 November 2026 · 7 min read

Design a business card in Canva that looks professional. This guide covers size, layout, fonts, colours, and print ready export.

You design a business card in Canva by picking the right size and adding your name and contact details. You learn how to design a business card in Canva with a simple layout that looks professional at first glance.

Most beginners crowd the card with many fonts and they fill every corner with text. They use tiny words that no one can read at arm length.

You finish this guide with a print ready card that shows your name, your work, and your contact details clearly. You hand it to clients with pride and you win calls from people who remember your card.

Set the Right Size and Layout First

You start when you learn how to design a business card in Canva by setting the business card size in Canva first. You open a blank card template and you check the size before you add any text. You keep the main details inside the safe centre and you leave clean space around the edges.

This size first habit works because a wrong size cuts off your text when the card goes to print. You avoid rework and you keep every word readable at arm length. You plan the front for your name and the back for your contact details so each side has one clear job.

Ayesha from Lahore shows this idea in action with a card for her henna art business. She sets the standard card size first and she places her name in the centre of the front. She leaves clean space for her phone number on the back and her first cards look sharp in print.

Choose Fonts and Colours That Fit Your Business

You choose business card fonts and colours by picking two fonts and three colours that match your work. You use one bold font for your name and one simple font for your contact details. You use the same colours on the front and the back so the card feels like one clean design.

This limit works because two fonts and three colours keep the card calm and easy to read. You test the colours at arm length and you check that the name stays clear against the background. You change any colour that feels dull and you keep only the colours that look clean and warm.

Bilal from Karachi shows this idea in action with a card for his mobile repair shop. He picks a dark blue colour and a white background with one bold font for his shop name. He prints a test card first and his customers read the phone number without any strain.

Add Your Name Contact Details and Services

You add your name, your phone number, and your main service when you learn how to design a business card in Canva for a real business. You keep the text short and you put the most useful detail in the largest type. You add one website address and you leave out details that no one needs on a small card.

This short list works because a card has room for only a few clear lines. You help the reader find your number in seconds and you stop them from throwing the card away. You place your main service under your name so the reader knows your work at once.

Sana from Islamabad shows this idea in action with a card for her home baking business. She adds her name, her phone number, and the words home baked cakes in large type. She adds her Instagram link on the back and her customers order cakes through that link every week.

Use Templates Without Making Every Card Look the Same

You save time when you open Canva business card templates for beginners and you change the design to fit your business. You pick one template with a clean layout and you replace the photo, the words, and the colours. You keep the layout and the fonts the same so the card stays neat and professional.

This method works because a template gives you speed and your changes give you a card that feels yours. You swap the main colour and you rewrite the headline for your service. You keep the brand colours in place and you add one fresh element that fits your business.

Usman from Faisalabad shows this idea in action with a template that he changes for his tailoring shop. He keeps the clean layout and he changes the colours to match his shop sign. He adds his phone number in large type and his first customers keep the card in their wallets.

Export the Card for Print and Check It First

You export business card from Canva for print by choosing the PDF print option with crop marks. You download the file and you open it on your computer to check every word. You print one test card at home and you hold it at arm length to check the size and the colours.

This check works because a test print shows problems that a screen hides from you. You spot tiny text, dull colours, and cut off edges before you pay for a full batch. You fix the file once and you order the print run with confidence that the result will look right.

Hira from Rawalpindi shows this idea in action with a card for her beauty parlour. She prints one test card and she finds that her phone number looks too small.
