How Beginners Design a Flyer in Canva for a Local Business

Graphic Design · 10 November 2026 · 7 min read

Design a flyer in Canva for a local business: pick the right size, use a clean template, write short text and export a print-ready file.

You can design a flyer in Canva for a local business in one evening. You pick a template, change the words and colours, and export a file ready to print. This guide shows you how to design a flyer in Canva for beginners step by step.

Most beginners fill the flyer with long paragraphs and five different fonts. Readers glance at it once and throw it away. They fail because they try to say everything instead of selling one clear offer.

After this guide you will hold a clean flyer that a local business can print and hand out with pride.

Pick the Right Flyer Size Before You Design

You pick the flyer size before you add any text or photo. You choose A5 for a handout and A4 for a notice on a wall. You learn how to design a flyer in Canva for beginners by starting with the size, not with the decoration.

You open Canva and search for the size you need, such as A5 flyer. You check that the size matches the paper your printer uses. You set the page to portrait when people will hold it in one hand. You keep the same size for every flyer in one campaign.

A designer in Lahore makes a flyer for a bakery sale and picks A5 size. She prints it on plain paper and hands it out near her shop. The size fits in a hand and readers keep it in a pocket. You can pick the right size first and your flyer will fit its job.

Start From a Clean Template and Make It Yours

You start from a clean Canva template instead of a blank page. You search for a flyer template that matches the business, such as food or tutoring. You pick a template with plenty of empty space and one strong photo area. You change the colours to match the business brand.

You keep the layout of the template and you change only the words and the photo. You delete any shape or icon that does not help the message. You keep one font for headings and one font for the small text. You save your version so you can reuse it for the next flyer.

A student in Karachi designs a flyer for a tuition centre with a Canva template. She keeps the layout and changes the name, the subjects and the phone number. She deletes a flower shape that clashes with the centre style. You can adapt a template the same way and keep the design clean.

Write Short Text That Sells the Offer

You write short text that names the offer in the first line. You say what the business gives, the price and the last date. You learn how to design a flyer in Canva for beginners by writing for a reader who glances once.

You put the headline in large letters at the top of the flyer. You add two or three short lines under it with the key details. You end with one action, such as call this number or visit the shop. You cut any word that does not help the reader decide.

A shop owner in Faisalabad writes a flyer for a winter sale with one headline. He adds the discount, the dates and his shop address in three short lines. He ends with his phone number in large letters. You can write short text the same way and readers will act on it fast.

Add Photos and Colours That Match the Business

You add one clear photo that shows the product or the service in real use. You place the photo where the eye lands first, near the top. You keep the photo large enough that the details show clearly. You crop the photo so nothing dull sits at the edges.

You pick two colours from the business brand and you use them across the flyer. You keep the background light so the text stays easy to read. You use the stronger colour only for the headline and the price. You check the flyer on a phone screen to confirm the colours still read well.

A designer in Multan makes a flyer for a juice bar with one bright photo. She uses green and orange from the shop sign across the whole flyer. She keeps the text dark on a light background so it reads fast. You can match the colours to the business the same way and the flyer will feel like part of the shop.

Export a Print-Ready File and Check It First

You export the flyer as a PDF when you plan to print it. You pick the print option in Canva so the colours stay strong on paper. You check the file size and you save a copy with the business name. You keep the Canva link so you can edit the flyer later.

You print one test copy before you print a full batch. You check the text for spelling errors and you check that no line sits too close to the edge. You look at the colours under shop light to confirm they match the screen. You fix any error you spot and you export the file again.

A student in Islamabad exports a flyer for a book fair as a print PDF. She prints one copy and spots a phone number with one wrong digit. She fixes the number in Canva and exports the file again. You can test one copy first and save the cost of a bad batch.

Can I print a Canva flyer at home?

What should I write on a business flyer?

You should write the offer first, then the price, the dates and one clear action. You should keep the text short so readers finish it in a few seconds. You should add the phone number or the shop address in large letters.

Can I use my own photos in a Canva flyer?

What file type should I export a flyer in?
