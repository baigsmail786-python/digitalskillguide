How Beginners Design a Business Card in Canva for a Small Business

Digitalskillguide · Graphic Design · 24 November 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer: You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners by gathering your details first and picking a template that fits your business. Canva gives you free tools to place text and images, and you export a sharp print file.

Most beginners cram every detail onto one card, and the crowded card leaves no room for the main message. People glance once and walk away, because they cannot find the phone number or the website.

You will make a card that shows your name, your work, and your contact details at a glance. You will print it with pride and share it online with ease.

Gather Your Details Before You Open Canva

You gather every detail before you open Canva, because a card built on wrong details wastes the whole print run. You write your name, your role, your phone number, your email, and your website on one page of your notebook. A bakery owner in Lahore checked her details twice this way, and her cards carried the right number from the first batch.

List the Details the Card Must Carry

You list the must have details first: your name, your business name, one phone number, one email, and your website. You add a short line about what you do, and you keep it under ten words. You leave out your full address unless clients visit you, and you leave out every social handle except the one that matters.

Add a Logo and a QR Code

You upload your logo as a PNG file with a clear background, and you place it where the eye lands first. You add a QR code that points to your website or booking page, and you test it with your own phone before you print. A salon owner in Faisalabad added a QR code to her card, and new clients booked from the card the same week.

Pick a Template That Fits the Business

This guide shows how to design a business card in Canva for beginners through template choice, because the right template does half the work for you. You search for Business Cards in Canva, and you filter the results by your industry and your style. You pick a template with a clear layout and room for your words, and you skip any template that crowds the card. A tailor in Rawalpindi picked a plain template for his shop, and his card looked neat enough to hang in his window.

Choose a Template by Industry and Mood

You filter templates by industry first, and you look for a style that matches how your clients see your work. You pick a bold template for a creative trade, and you pick a calm template for a serious trade like accounting. You check the template at full size, and you make sure the name stands out from the rest.

Set Brand Colours and Two Fonts

You swap the template colours for your own brand colours, and you enter each colour with its hex code for an exact match. You pick one font for your name and a second font for the details, and you never use a third font on one card. A florist in Multan set her brand green and one clean font across her cards, and her cards now match her shop sign. You gain a card that looks like your business, and you keep the text easy to read.

Place Text and Images With a Clear Order

You place the biggest text first and the details last, because order guides the eye across a small card. You set your name in the biggest text, and you set your role and contact details in smaller text below it. You leave empty space around each part, and the space helps each part stand out on its own.

Make Your Name the Biggest Text

You set your name larger than every other line on the card, because your name is the one thing people must remember. You keep the role line short, and you place it right under the name in a smaller size. You use a readable font for the contact details, even when your logo font looks fancy.

Use the Back for One Clear Offer

You use the back of the card for one clear offer or a short line about your work, and you never cram the back with a full menu. A cafe owner in Karachi put a free drink offer on the back, and customers kept the card to claim it later. Check Margins and Print With Confidence

You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners best when you check the print settings before you order, because a fine design still fails with a bad export. You turn on margins in Canva, and you keep every word and your logo inside the safe zone. You stretch the background colour into the bleed when it must reach the edge, and you keep text away from the trim line. You export the card as a PDF Print file, and you check the file on your phone before you print.

Turn On Margins and Bleed

You open File, then Settings, then Show margins in Canva, and a broken line marks the safe zone on your card. You keep all text and your logo inside that line, because the print shop trims the edge after printing. You stretch a background colour past the edge into the bleed, and Canva adds an eighth of an inch on each side for the trim. You gain a card that prints exactly as you designed it, and you lose no text to the cutter.

Order One Test Card First

You order a single test copy first when you feel unsure, and you check the card in your hand before you order a full batch. You read every phone digit out loud, and you zoom to twice the size to check your logo for blur. You fix any line that touches the edge, and you export again before you send the file to print. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Canva free for business card design?

Canva free gives you templates, fonts, and images that suit most small business cards. You pay only when you want Pro fonts or Pro images, and the free set covers most jobs. You start with the free plan, and you upgrade only when you need a Pro item.

What size should a business card be in Canva?

You use the standard card size of three and a half inches wide and two inches tall in Canva. You set the size before you add text, because a size change later moves your whole design. Canva lists the common sizes in its menu, and the business card size sits there ready to pick.

How do I design a business card in Canva for beginners?

You learn how to design a business card in Canva for beginners by gathering your details first and picking a template that fits your business. You place the big text first and the details last, and you export the result as a PDF Print file. The plan keeps the card clear, and the export keeps it sharp.

Can I print a Canva business card at a print shop?

You can print a Canva card at any print shop, and you send the shop a PDF Print file. You tick the crop marks box when the shop asks for it, and you check the file on your phone first. The shop prints from your file, so a clean file gives a clean print.

How many fonts should I use on a business card?

You use two fonts at most on one card, and you give the main message one font and the details the other. Three fonts make the card look messy, and one font can look flat. You test the pair from a few steps back, and you keep the pair that reads clear.

What file type is best for a printed business card?

PDF Print is the best file type for a printed card, because it keeps the text sharp at any size. You export a phone version as a PNG file, and you keep the resolution high. You check the file before you print, and you fix any line that touches the edge.

How do I add a QR code to my business card in Canva?

You open the Apps menu in Canva, and you search for the QR Code app. You enter the web address you want the code to open, and Canva adds the code to your card. You test the code with your own phone, and you move it where the eye can find it.

How do I make the text easy to read on a business card?

You make the text easy to read by using big text for your name and smaller text for the details. You leave empty space around each part, and you pick colours that stand apart from the background. You check the card from a few steps back, and the big line should still read clear.
