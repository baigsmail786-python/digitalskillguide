How Beginners Design a Menu in Canva for a Small Cafe or Restaurant

Digitalskillguide · Graphic Design · 27 November 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer: You learn how to design a menu in Canva for beginners by gathering your dishes and prices first, then changing a template to fit them. You keep the text short, and you export a PDF for print and a PNG for sharing.

Most beginners open Canva first and type dishes into a template, and the menu ends up crowded with tiny text. They then print it without a check, and customers struggle to read the prices.

You will build a menu for a cafe, and you will check it on paper and on a phone. You will export it for both and reuse the layout for a season.

Gather Your Menu Content Before You Open Canva

You gather your menu content before you open Canva, because a menu designed around real dishes reads better than one filled in later. You list every dish with its price, and you check each price with the owner before you type it. A cafe owner in Lahore gathered her menu this way, and the design took half the time it used to take.

List Every Dish With Its Price

You write every dish on one page first, and you put the price beside each name in plain numbers. You check the spelling of each dish, and you fix any name that customers misread. You mark the dishes that sell best, and you give them a clear spot near the top of their section. You share the list with the owner, and you get a yes on every price before you design.

Clean Up the Wording and Group the Dishes

You clean up the wording next, and you shorten each dish description to five words or fewer. You group the dishes into sections such as breakfast, mains, drinks, and desserts, and you give each section a clear heading. You read the list aloud once, and you cut any word that adds nothing to the dish.

Pick the Right Size and a Template That Fits

You pick the right size before you touch a template, because the size decides how the menu prints and how it reads on a phone. You use A4 for a printed menu in most towns, and you use US Letter where printers expect it. A restaurant in Islamabad picked A4 for its table menu, and the print matched the design exactly.

Choose A4 for Print and a Tall Shape for Phones

You open Canva, and you type menu in the search box to see the size options. You pick A4 when the menu will sit on a table or a counter, and you pick a tall phone shape when it will travel on WhatsApp. You check the size in the top bar before you add anything, and you fix it at once when it looks wrong. You save the size as your starting point, and you build every later menu on the same size.

Start From a Template or a Blank Page

You search templates by your cafe, such as a coffee shop, a bakery, or a family restaurant. You pick one template with a clear headline spot, and you change the photo first. You start from a blank page when templates pull you into endless browsing, and you add one photo and one headline yourself. Design Sections Prices and Fonts So the Menu Reads Clearly

You learn how to design a menu in Canva for beginners best when you group dishes into clear sections and align the prices in one clean column. You give each section a heading, and you keep the dishes in the order customers read them. You set the prices in a straight line, and the eye moves down the menu without effort. A school cafe in Lahore follows this rule for every menu, and parents read the prices without squinting.

Group Dishes Into Clear Sections

You group dishes into sections that match how customers order, such as starters, mains, drinks, and desserts. You give each section a short heading, and you place the heading in a larger size than the dish names. You keep each section to the dishes it owns, and you move a dish when it fits another section better. You leave space between sections, and the menu gains a calm order that readers follow at a glance.

Align Prices So the Menu Looks Professional

You align every price in one straight column on the right, and the menu gains a clean edge that looks professional. You use the same number of decimal places for every price, and you keep the currency mark in the same spot each time. You check the column from two steps away, and you fix any price that sits out of line.

Check the Menu on Paper and on a Phone Then Export It

You check the menu before you export it, because a check takes two minutes and a reprint costs far more. You print one test copy first, and you read it from two steps away. A community cafe in Faisalabad caught a wrong price this way, and the fix took one minute instead of a fresh print run.

Run a Print and Phone Check

You print one test copy, and you read every word from two steps away. You check the date, the prices, and the phone number twice, because these three details cause most menu failures. You open the design on your phone, and you check that the dish names read clearly at small size. You learn how to design a menu in Canva for beginners best when you run this check on every menu before you export it.

Export a PDF for Print and a PNG for Sharing

You click Share and then Download, and you pick PDF Print when the menu goes to a printer. You pick PNG when the menu will travel on WhatsApp or social media, because PNG keeps the text crisp on a phone. You name the file clearly, and you keep the design saved in Canva for the next season. Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a menu be in Canva?

Do I need Canva Pro to design a menu?

You do not need Canva Pro to design a good menu, because the free plan carries thousands of templates and photos. You start with the free plan, and you learn its tools first. You upgrade only when you need a brand kit or a background remover every week.

How do I align prices on a Canva menu?

You keep the currency mark in the same spot each time, and you check the column from two steps away. You fix any price that sits out of line, and the menu looks professional at once.

Should I add photos to my cafe menu?

You add photos to a cafe menu when the photos show your real dishes in good light. You place one photo near the section it belongs to, and you keep faces and plates clear and bright. You skip a photo when it looks blurry or fake, and the real dishes build more trust than a generic image.

How do I add a QR code to a Canva menu?

You add a QR code in Canva by opening the Apps section and choosing a QR code tool. You paste the link to your order form or your WhatsApp chat, and Canva places the code on your menu. You test the code with your own phone before you print, and you make it large enough to scan from a short distance.

Can I design a menu in Canva on my phone?

You can design a menu in Canva on your phone, because the app carries the same templates and tools as the desktop version. You pick the size, and you drag the photo and the text into place with your finger. You check the menu at full size before you export it, because small screens hide small mistakes.
