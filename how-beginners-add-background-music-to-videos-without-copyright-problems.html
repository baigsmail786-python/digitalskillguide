Add Music in CapCut and Balance the Volume

You add music in CapCut for beginners by importing your video and tapping Audio, then picking the track from your phone. You place the track under your voice on the timeline and you trim it to match the video length. You add a short fade at the start and the end so the music never cuts off hard.

Balanced volume works because the viewer hears your words first and the music stays in the background. You lower the music to about one fifth of the voice level and you test it on your phone speaker. You check the mix with headphones too and you fix any part where the music covers a word.
