How Beginners Design a Price List in Canva for a Small Business

Graphic Design · 21 November 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer: You design a price list in Canva by picking a clean template, listing each service with one clear price, and keeping the layout calm. You use two fonts at most, and you leave white space around each row.

Most beginners cram every service onto one crowded page, and the buyer cannot find the price they need. This habit hides the offer, because a crowded list tires the eye before it sells.

You will finish this guide with a price list you can print today. You will know the right size, the right layout, and the right export for your shop.

Pick a Template and Set the Right Size

Learning how to create a price list in Canva for beginners starts with a template, because a clean template gives you the layout first. You open Canva, search for price list, and pick a template with clear rows. You check the size first, and you set A4 when you plan to print. The template holds your services in neat rows, and you fill them in one by one.

Search for price list templates

You type price list into the Canva search box, and Canva shows you templates for salons, cafes, shops, and services. You scroll past the busy ones, and you pick a template with one column for services and one for prices. The simple template leaves room for your colours and your logo. You click it once, and Canva opens a fresh design you can change.

Set A4 size for printing

You set the page to A4 size when you plan to print the list, because A4 fits the printers in most shops. You find the size setting at the top, and you type the A4 numbers before you add any text. The right size keeps your rows from crowding, and the print comes out sharp. You keep the same size for every list, so buyers learn where to look.

List Services With Clear Prices

Learning how to create a price list in Canva for beginners moves to the list itself next, because the services and the prices carry the whole page. You write each service on its own row, and you place one clear price at the end. You order the rows from the most wanted service down, and you keep the names short. The buyer scans down the rows, and the price they need jumps out at once.

Write each service on its own row

You type the service name on the left of the row, and you type the price on the right in the same line. The two columns line up down the page, and the eye moves from name to price without a search. You never write two services in one row, because the row then confuses the eye. A salon in Multan listed each service on its own row, and buyers found their price fast.

Keep prices clear and honest

You write the full price with the currency, and you add from only when the price truly varies. The clear price builds trust at once, because the buyer knows the cost before they call. You never hide the price behind a call us line, because the buyer moves to a shop that shows it. Clear prices pair with clear words, so read How Beginners Write Product Descriptions That Sell Online when you want your product words to sell too.

Use Fonts and Colours That Match Your Business

Learning how to create a price list in Canva for beginners moves to fonts and colours next, because the look tells the buyer what kind of business you run. You pick one font for the names and one plain font for the prices, and you use no third one. You pick two colours from your shop sign for the title and the lines. The calm look reads as a real business, and the buyer trusts the prices.

Use two fonts at most

You set the names in one strong font, and you set the prices in one plain font that reads clear at a small size. The two fonts give the page a calm order, and the buyer reads down the rows without a pause. You never add a third font for a heading, because the third font fights the other two. The two font rule works for any page, and it keeps your brand looking steady.

Match colours to your business

You pick colours that match your shop, such as green for a calm salon or red for a food shop. The match tells the buyer the list belongs to your business, and it builds a memory. You keep the background white or pale, and you use the strong colour only for the title and the lines. A cafe in Lahore used its sign colour on the list, and buyers knew the page at a glance.

Export and Share the Finished List

Learning how to create a price list in Canva for beginners ends with the export, because the file must print sharp and share clean. You download the list as a PDF to print it, and you pick PNG to share it on WhatsApp. You open the file once, and you check each price and each spelling before you send it. The final check saves you from a printed error, and it keeps the list honest.

Export a PDF for print

You export a PDF when you plan to print, because printers read that format without shifting the layout. You click Share, then Download, and you pick PDF Print from the list. Canva saves the file, and you open it to check the rows still line up. You print one test copy first, and you fix any row that looks off before you print the rest.

Share the list on WhatsApp and in your shop

Learning how to create a price list in Canva for beginners helps you share the list too, because a list on your computer earns nothing. You send the PNG to buyers on WhatsApp, and you pin it in your shop. You also post it on your social page, and you update it there when a price moves. Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a price list be in Canva?

A4 size works best for a printed price list, because it fits the printers in most shops and reads clear in the hand. You set A4 before you add any text, and you keep the same size for every list you make. You use a phone screen size only when the list lives on WhatsApp and never goes to print.

Can I design a price list in Canva for free?

You can design a full price list with the free Canva plan, because the free templates, fonts, and shapes cover the job. You open a price list template, change the words and the colours, and export it without paying. The paid plan adds more images, but a beginner rarely needs them for a price list.

How many services should a price list show?

You show the services buyers ask for most, and you keep the list to one page when you can. A long list tires the eye, so you group small extras under one line or move them to a second page. You pick the services that earn you money, and you leave the rest for a chat with the buyer.

Should I show prices or ask buyers to call?

You show the price when it stays fixed, because buyers trust a shop that shows its prices. You ask buyers to call only when the price truly changes with the job, such as a custom order. You write from beside the price when the price varies, and the buyer still sees the range.

What font works best for a price list?

A plain font works best for the prices, because the buyer must read the numbers at a glance. You pair it with one strong font for the service names, and you use no third font anywhere. You test the page at arm length, and you grow the size when the numbers blur.

How do I update prices on a Canva price list?

You open the design in Canva, and you click the old price to type the new one in its place. You check the rows after the change, because a longer number can push a row out of line. You export the file again, and you replace the old file wherever you shared it.

Can I print a Canva price list at home?

You can print the list at home when you export it as a PDF, because the PDF keeps the layout sharp on any printer. You pick A4 size and the best print quality, and you print one test copy first. You check the test copy for cut off edges, and you fix the margins before you print the rest.

Should a salon price list look different from a shop price list?

A salon list groups services by treatment, such as hair, skin, and nails, while a shop list groups items by type, such as food, drinks, and deals. The layout stays the same, with one row per service and one clear price. You change only the colours and the groups, because the buyer reads both lists the same way.
