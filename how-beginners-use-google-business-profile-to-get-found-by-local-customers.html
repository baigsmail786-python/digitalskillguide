How Beginners Use Google Business Profile to Get Found by Local Customers

Digitalskillguide · Digital Marketing · 25 November 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer: You learn how to set up Google Business Profile for beginners by signing in with a Google account, adding your real name and category, and verifying the business is yours. The profile is free, and it shows your business on Google Search and Maps.

Most beginners stuff keywords into the business name, and Google suspends the profile for it. They then wait weeks for a review, when the real name would go live in days.

You will finish a profile in one sitting, and you will follow a first week routine that brings your first calls. You will then keep it fresh with photos and posts each week.

Gather Everything Before You Open the Form

You gather everything first, because hunting for details halfway through turns a thirty minute job into a three day job. You write your exact business name as it appears on your sign and your invoices, and you note your address, your phone number, your website, and your opening hours in one note. You also pick five to ten photos that show your shop, your team, and your work in action.

Write Your Real Name and Pick One Category

You type your real business name exactly as customers know it, and you leave out keywords, locations, and taglines. You pick one main category that best describes your business, such as bakery, dental clinic, or hair salon. You add extra categories later when the main one fits, and you never pick a category just because it sounds popular. You check the spelling twice, because a wrong name slows down verification.

Keep Your Phone Number and Hours Ready

You keep a phone number that customers can call and that someone answers during working hours, because Google may use it to verify the business. You write your opening hours for each day, and you note holiday hours separately so customers never arrive at a closed door. You keep the same hours on your website and your social pages, and the match builds trust with Google and with customers.

Create the Profile and Verify That the Business Is Yours

You learn how to set up Google Business Profile for beginners best when you follow the steps in order and verify at once. You visit the Google Business Profile page, sign in with the Google account that will own the profile, and click the button to manage a profile. You check the name and the category once more before you move on.

Add Your Location or Service Area

You add your full address when customers visit you, and you add the towns you cover when you visit customers instead. You hide the address from the public view when you run a service area business, and you still enter it for verification. You never claim a location you do not serve, because Google treats a false location as a reason to suspend the profile. You check the map pin once, and you move it to the right door when it sits in the wrong place.

Choose a Verification Method and Finish It

Google asks you to verify the business before the profile goes fully live, and Google decides which method you get. You may get a text message, a phone call, an email, a video, or a postcard to your address. You follow the method at once, and you keep the code safe until the profile shows as verified. You never pay anyone who promises to verify a profile for you, because verification is free and only Google grants it.

Complete the Profile So Customers Choose You

You complete the profile after verification, because an empty profile rarely wins a call. You write a short description of up to 750 characters that says what you do and who you serve, and you add your services, your hours, and your photos. You add a photo of the outside of your shop first, so customers recognise the place when they arrive. Read How Beginners Set Up a Google Business Profile for a Small Business for a second look at the setup steps before you publish your profile.

Add Photos That Show Real Work

You add photos that show your real shop, your real team, and your real work, and you skip stock images that could belong to anyone. You mix exterior shots, interior shots, and photos of your team at work, and you add new photos each month so the profile stays fresh. You name each photo clearly when you upload it, and you check that it looks sharp on a phone screen. You remove any photo that looks blurry or out of date.

Write Services and a Clear Description

You list each service you offer with its own short line, and you keep the wording plain so customers understand it at a glance. You write the description in your own words, and you say what makes your business worth a visit without stuffing it with keywords. You check the description on a phone, and you shorten any sentence that feels long. You update the services whenever you add a new one.

Follow a First Week Routine After the Profile Goes Live

You follow a first week routine after the profile goes live, because setup alone brings no calls. You ask three happy customers to leave a review, and you reply to each review with a short thank you that names the service they used. You post one update about a new dish, a new service, or a new offer, and you check the profile each morning for questions from customers.

Ask for Reviews and Answer Every One

You ask for a review right after a happy customer leaves, because the memory is fresh and the answer comes fast. You send the review link by message, and you never offer a reward for a review. You answer every review within a day, and you thank the reviewer by name for the kind words. You answer a poor review calmly and offer to fix the problem, and future customers read your reply as proof of your care.

Post Once a Week and Keep Hours True

You post once a week about a new product, a new service, or a useful tip, and you add one photo to each post. You check your hours after every holiday, and you update them before the holiday starts. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Google Business Profile?

A Google Business Profile is the free listing that controls how your business appears on Google Search and Google Maps. You manage your name, address, hours, photos, services, and reviews from one place. You use it when you want nearby customers to find you and call you.

Is a Google Business Profile free?

A Google Business Profile is free to create, manage, and update for every eligible business. You pay nothing to Google for the listing, and you pay nothing for verification. You avoid anyone who charges a fee to set it up for you.

How long does verification take?

Verification time depends on the method Google gives you, and a text or call code arrives in minutes. A postcard takes days to reach your address, and a video review takes longer still. You start the method at once, and you keep the code safe until the profile shows as verified.

What should I put in my business description?

You write a short description of up to 750 characters that says what you do and who you serve. You use plain words, and you say what makes your business worth a visit. You leave out keyword stuffing, because Google suspends profiles that stuff keywords into the name or the description.

How many photos should I add to my profile?

You add five to ten photos at the start, and you add new photos each month after that. You mix exterior shots, interior shots, and photos of your team at work. You skip stock images, and you remove any photo that looks blurry or out of date.

Can I run a business without a shop address?

You can run a Google Business Profile as a service area business when you visit customers instead of receiving them. You enter your address for verification, and you hide it from the public view. You add the towns you cover, and you never claim a location you do not serve.

How do I get my first Google reviews?

You ask three happy customers to leave a review right after a good visit, and you send them the review link by message. You never offer a reward for a review, and you answer every review within a day. You thank each reviewer by name, and you fix any problem a poor review raises.

How do I learn to set up Google Business Profile as a beginner?

You learn how to set up Google Business Profile for beginners by gathering your details first and following the steps in order. You add your real name and one category, and you verify at once. You then complete the profile with photos and services, and you follow a first week routine that brings your first calls.
