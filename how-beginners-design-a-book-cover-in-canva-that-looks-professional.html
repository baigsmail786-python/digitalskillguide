How Beginners Design a Book Cover in Canva That Looks Professional

Graphic Design · 20 November 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer: You design a book cover in Canva by setting the right size first, then you build a layout that puts the title where the eye lands. You pick fonts and colours that fit the genre, and you test the cover at thumbnail size. You then export the file in the format your printer or store asks for.

You design a book cover in Canva by setting the size first and building the title around it. The cover earns the click on a store page, and a clear title at thumbnail size wins the sale.

Most beginners fill the cover with art and squeeze the title into a corner, and the book looks dull beside the rest. This habit hides the title, because the art wins the space the title needs.

You will finish this guide with a cover you can upload today. You will know the right size, the right layout, and the right export for your book.

Set the Right Size Before You Design Anything

Learning how to design a book cover in Canva for beginners starts with size, because a cover built at the wrong size turns blurry or crops the title. You set the size before you add a single word, and you pick the size that matches where the book will sell. An ebook cover uses a tall portrait shape, while a paperback needs the exact trim size from your printer.

Use 1600 by 2560 pixels for an ebook cover

You open Canva with a custom size of 1600 by 2560 pixels, because that tall shape fits the Amazon KDP ebook cover slot. The ratio keeps the title large and the art clear on a phone screen, and it matches what most stores expect. You set the size once at the start, and you never stretch the design later.

Check the print size when you plan a paperback

You ask your printer for the trim size and the spine width before you design a paperback cover. The trim size sets the height and the width of the front, and the spine width follows the page count of your book. You build the full wrap in Canva with those numbers, and you keep the title and the art clear of the fold lines.

Choose a Layout That Puts the Title First

The title sells the book, so the layout must give the title the best spot on the cover. Learning how to design a book cover in Canva for beginners means you place the title where the eye lands first, and you let the art support it. You sketch the layout on paper before you open Canva, and you mark the title, the art, and the author name in three zones.

Place the title where the eye lands first

You place the title in the top third of the cover, because the eye starts there on a store page. Learning how to design a book cover in Canva for beginners feels easy once the title sits in that spot, and the large title reads clear even at thumbnail size. You keep the title to a few words per line, and you break a long title across two lines with care.

Keep the author name small and low

You set the author name in small type near the bottom, because the title must win the first glance. A new author gains nothing from a large name, while a known author can grow the name a little. The small name still reads clear and leaves the cover calm, and logo design follows the same title first habit, so read How Beginners Design A Logo In Canva That Looks Professional for more layout ideas.

Pick Fonts and Colours That Fit the Genre

The genre tells the reader what kind of book this is, so the fonts and colours must speak that language. Learning how to design a book cover in Canva for beginners means you study ten covers in your genre first, and you note the fonts and colours they share. A thriller uses dark tones and sharp type, while a romance uses soft colours and flowing type.

Use one font for the title and one for the author

You pick one strong font for the title and one plain font for the author name, and you use no third font anywhere on the cover. Two fonts keep the cover calm, while three fonts fight for attention and tire the eye. You test the title font at thumbnail size, and you swap it when the letters blur together.

Test the cover at thumbnail size

You shrink the cover to the size of a phone thumbnail, because most readers meet your book that small first. The title must still read clear, and the art must still make sense at that size. A writer in Multan grew her title after the thumbnail test, and her cover finally stood out beside the rest, so the same test lifts social posts too, and you can read How Beginners Design Instagram Posts In Canva That Look Professional for more cover ideas.

Export the Cover in the Right Format

The export step turns your design into a file the store or the printer can use, so you pick the format they ask for. Learning how to design a book cover in Canva for beginners ends with a clean export, and the file must stay sharp at full size. You download the ebook cover as a high quality PNG, and you export a paperback cover as a PDF.

Download a high quality PNG for an ebook

You download the ebook cover as a PNG file, because that format keeps the text sharp and the colours true. Canva lets you pick the size on download, and you keep the full 1600 by 2560 pixels for the store. You open the downloaded file once and check the title at full size, because a blurry title at this stage means a fix in the design. The quick check saves you from uploading a cover you would soon replace.

Export a PDF when the printer asks for one

You export a paperback cover as a PDF, because printers read that format without shifting the layout. You pick the PDF print option in Canva, and you keep the colour mode the printer asked for. You also check the bleed and the safe margins before you send the file, because text near the edge can vanish in the trim. The printer then prints the cover you designed, and the book arrives looking like the proof you approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a book cover be in Canva?

An ebook cover works well at 1600 by 2560 pixels, because that tall shape fits the Amazon KDP cover slot. You set the custom size before you add any text, and you never stretch the design later. A paperback cover needs the exact trim size and spine width from your printer.

Can I design a book cover in Canva for free?

You can design a full cover with the free Canva plan, because the free fonts and images cover most needs. You open a custom size design, build the cover, and export it as a PNG without paying. The paid plan adds more images, but a beginner rarely needs them at the start.

What makes a book cover look professional?

A professional cover gives the title the best spot, uses two fonts at most, and matches the colours of its genre. You test the cover at thumbnail size, because most readers meet it that small first. The clean layout and the clear title make the book look like a real store title.

How many fonts should a book cover use?

You use two fonts at most on a cover, with one strong font for the title and one plain font for the author name. A third font fights for attention, and it tires the eye fast. You test the title font at thumbnail size, and you swap it when the letters blur together.

Should the author name be large on a cover?

You keep the author name small and low on the cover, because the title must win the first glance. A new author gains nothing from a large name, while a known author can grow the name a little. You pick a plain font for the name, and you keep it smaller than the title.

What file format is best for a KDP book cover?

You upload an ebook cover to KDP as a JPG or PNG file at 1600 by 2560 pixels, because that size keeps the title sharp. You export a paperback cover as a PDF, because printers read that format without shifting the layout. You check the file once at full size before you upload it.

Can I use Canva images on a book cover I sell?

You can use Canva images on a cover you sell when the image licence allows it, so you read the licence beside each image. Free images allow most uses, while some pro images need the paid plan. You check the rule once, and you keep a note of it with your book files.

How do I test a book cover before I publish it?

You shrink the cover to thumbnail size and check that the title still reads clear. You also show it to three readers in your genre and ask what kind of book they expect. The thumbnail test and the reader test together show you whether the cover sells the right book.
