You plan the intro first when you learn how to make a YouTube intro for beginners with a clear message. You check the YouTube intro length for beginners and you keep the intro under ten seconds. You write one line that names your channel and the promise of your videos.

Add Your Logo and Channel Name

You add logo to YouTube intro by placing it in the centre of the first frame. You add your channel name under the logo in clear letters. You keep the logo large enough to read on a phone and you leave clean space around it.

A clear logo works because viewers link the intro to your channel in one glance. You build a brand that viewers recall when they see your next video. You use the same logo in every video and you keep the size and the place the same.
